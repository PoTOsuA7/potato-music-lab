Privacy Policy

Potato Music Lab Uploader · Last updated 14 November 2026

Summary

Potato Music Lab Uploader is a personal tool used only by the Potato Music Lab creator on their own computer. It collects no data from viewers or other people, has no servers, and shares nothing with anyone.

What the app uses from TikTok

  • Basic account info (user.info.basic): the account's display name, to confirm the right account is signed in.
  • Video upload and publishing (video.upload, video.publish): to upload the creator's own videos and captions.
  • Creator posting settings: the privacy options TikTok allows for the account, so each post uses a valid one.

Where it is stored

The sign-in tokens TikTok issues are saved only in a private file on the creator's own computer. They are never uploaded, sold or shared. Nothing else is stored apart from a local list of which videos were posted.

Other people's data

The app does not read, collect or store any information about followers, viewers or other TikTok users.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions). Deleting the local file removes the stored tokens from the computer.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the date at the top shows the latest version.
