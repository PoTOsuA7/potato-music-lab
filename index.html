Potato Music Lab

Music secrets, explained by a potato. Real theory, one short video at a time.

What this is

Potato Music Lab makes short videos that answer one music question each: why the black keys on a piano come in groups of 2 and 3, why minor chords sound sad, why so many hit songs use the same four chords, and more.

Potato Music Lab Uploader

The Uploader is a small personal tool that runs on the creator's own computer. It uploads Potato Music Lab videos, with their captions, to the creator's own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API. It is not offered to other users.
