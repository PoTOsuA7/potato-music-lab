1. What the app does

Potato Music Lab Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool that uploads videos made by Potato Music Lab to the Potato Music Lab TikTok account, using TikTok's official Content Posting API. It runs only on the creator's own computer.

2. Who can use it

The app is for the Potato Music Lab creator's own account only. It is not offered or sold to anyone else.

3. TikTok's rules

Every upload must follow TikTok's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines and Developer Terms. The app does not change, get around or automate anything beyond what TikTok's API allows.

4. Content

All uploaded videos are original Potato Music Lab content. Third-party material (such as the Salamander Grand Piano samples, CC BY 3.0) is credited in each post as its license requires.

5. Access

The app only acts after the account owner signs in through TikTok's own login page and approves it. Access can be removed at any time in TikTok's settings (Security → Manage app permissions).
